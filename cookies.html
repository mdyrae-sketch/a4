Cookie Architecture & Preference Charter

Effective Date: January 2025 • Registered Mill: 520 Broadway, Suite 400, New York, NY 10012

1. Overview of Cookie Architecture

CottonKnitPod uses cookies, pixel tags, and local storage tokens to deliver an intuitive, responsive, and secure browsing experience across our digital atelier. This Cookie Charter details the specific types of cookies we deploy, their operational lifespan, and how you can exercise full control over your preferences.

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by web servers when you navigate our site. They assist us in recognizing your device, maintaining your custom Sock Architect configurations across sessions, and analyzing aggregated user journeys to optimize web page speed.

2. Categories of Cookies Deployed on CottonKnitPod

We classify our web cookies into four distinct operational tiers:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for the fundamental functioning of our website. These cookies manage security tokens, maintain active shopping cart contents, and remember your interface state (such as custom Sock Architect configurations). These cookies cannot be disabled without impairing core functionality.
  • Preference & Theme Cookies: Store your user-selected interface settings, including your preference for Daylight Loom Mode vs. Twilight Loom Night Mode (persisted via `ckp_theme` in localStorage).
  • Analytical & Performance Cookies: Gather anonymous, aggregated telemetry regarding how visitors interact with our pages, which technical monographs receive the highest engagement, and site loading speeds. We utilize Google Analytics (Tag ID: G-0LY0HY7L01) with IP anonymization enabled.
  • Security and Anti-Fraud Cookies: Assist in verifying legitimate customer sessions and protecting our payment processing gateways against automated brute-force attempts.

4. User Preference Management & Browser Controls

You have full autonomy over the management of cookies. Most modern web browsers allow you to inspect, block, or delete cookies through their built-in privacy settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies.

Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may prevent the Sock Architect tool and checkout from operating correctly.

6. Local Storage & Session State Architecture

In addition to standard HTTP cookies, CottonKnitPod utilizes modern HTML5 Local Storage APIs to persist client-side interface configurations without transmitting redundant network headers with every page request. The `ckp_theme` token resides entirely on your local device to ensure instant, flicker-free rendering of your chosen Daylight Loom Mode or Twilight Loom Night Mode upon navigation.

Similarly, the interactive Sock Architect tool utilizes temporary browser memory caching to store active silhouette choices, needle gauge options, and custom yarn color selections. This local state data is non-identifying and can be cleared at any time by emptying your browser's local application cache.

7. Do-Not-Track (DNT) and Global Privacy Control (GPC) Signals

CottonKnitPod recognizes and honors browser-level Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals and Do-Not-Track (DNT) headers. When our web servers detect a valid GPC or DNT signal from your browser client, non-essential analytical cookies (including Google Analytics session telemetry) are automatically suppressed, ensuring your visit remains strictly limited to essential functional and preference cookies.

5. Updates to this Cookie Charter

We may periodically update this Cookie Charter to reflect modifications in our technology stack, regulatory requirements, or service offerings. Any alterations will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
