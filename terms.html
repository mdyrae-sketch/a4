2. Artisanal Craftsmanship and Natural Fiber Variances

Every pair of CottonKnitPod socks is constructed utilizing natural organic fibers, including 100% GOTS certified combed organic cotton, botanical dyed slub yarns, and fine merino wool blends. Because natural plant and animal fibers possess organic microscopic characteristics, slight variations in yarn texture, heathered color depth, and natural botanical dye hue are inherent attributes of authentic small-batch textile production and shall not be classified as manufacturing defects.

We take extraordinary care in spinning and circular knitting, but slight tonal nuances across seasonal agricultural crop yields represent the authentic beauty of natural, chemical-free textiles.

9. Custom Batch Tolerances & Knitting Gauge Disclosures

Clients commissioning custom runs via our Sock Architect tool acknowledge that circular knitting machines operate under dynamic mechanical yarn tension. Dimensional measurements (cuff height, foot length, and rib width) possess an allowable manufacturing tolerance of plus or minus five percent (+/- 5%) in unstretched state. This variation is characteristic of high-needle-count circular knitting and is neutralized once the garment adapts to the wearer's foot anatomy upon initial wear.

Furthermore, custom yarn blends incorporating natural botanical dyes may exhibit subtle shade differences across separate dye vat runs. Patrons requiring strict color matching for uniform corporate capsules are advised to commission their entire order within a single unified dye lot batch.
