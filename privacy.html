CottonKnitPod Governance

Privacy Charter & Data Governance

Effective Date: January 2025 • Registered Mill: 520 Broadway, Suite 400, New York, NY 10012

1. Commitment to Customer Privacy & Confidentiality

At CottonKnitPod (operated under CottonKnitPod Mill & Atelier, 520 Broadway, Suite 400, New York, NY 10012, United States), we hold the privacy and personal security of our patrons, website visitors, and textile partners in the highest regard. This Privacy Charter articulates our comprehensive protocols regarding the collection, processing, storage, and protection of information gathered through your interactions with our digital platforms.

We operate under strict global privacy standards, adhering to the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), and related international data governance frameworks. We maintain an uncompromising policy: we never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers or marketing conglomerates.

Our digital infrastructure has been engineered from the ground up with data minimization principles. We collect only the precise information necessary to knit, customize, and deliver your orders, maintain secure server operations, and uphold our lifetime craftsmanship guarantees.

2. Categories of Information Collected

To provide our artisanal sock manufacturing services, process online orders, and facilitate custom knitting consultations, we collect specific categories of information:

  • Direct Contact Identification: Full legal name, billing address, dispatch and shipping address, email address, telephone number, and communication preferences.
  • Transactional and Billing Information: Payment method tokens processed via PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateways. We do not store raw card details or sensitive account credentials on our local servers.
  • Custom Sock Specifications: User-selected parameters submitted through our interactive Sock Architect tool, including silhouette height, needle gauge preferences, yarn color combinations, and foot sizing measurements.
  • Technical and Analytical Telemetry: Anonymized IP addresses, browser engine versions, device screen resolutions, referring URLs, time spent per page, and navigation paths collected via Google Analytics (Tag ID: G-0LY0HY7L01) solely for site performance optimization.
  • Patron Communications: Records of inquiries sent to our concierge desk, custom yarn batch requests, and feedback provided during fit consultations.

3. Lawful Basis and Purpose of Processing

CottonKnitPod processes your personal data strictly under valid lawful bases recognized by international data protection laws:

  1. Contractual Performance: Processing necessary to fulfill your garment orders, construct custom knitted batches, process shipping dispatches, and provide customer support.
  2. Legitimate Business Interests: Maintaining web server security, preventing fraudulent transactions, monitoring mill inventory flow, and optimizing our web interface usability.
  3. Explicit User Consent: When you voluntarily subscribe to our seasonal dispatch newsletters or consent to analytical cookies. You may revoke consent at any time.
  4. Legal Compliance: Retaining mandatory transactional invoices and fiscal records in accordance with United States federal and state commercial accounting requirements.

4. Data Retention and Lifecycle Management

Personal data is retained only for the duration necessary to achieve the specific purposes outlined in this charter, unless a longer statutory retention period is mandated by law. Transactional records are securely archived for seven years to satisfy commercial accounting obligations. Analytical telemetry data is automatically scrubbed and aggregated after 14 months.

When the retention period expires, data is permanently purged using certified cryptographic erasure methods that prevent reconstruction or unauthorized extraction.

5. Technical and Organizational Security Protocols

CottonKnitPod implements enterprise-grade technical safeguards to defend your data against unauthorized access, destruction, alteration, or disclosure. All data transmissions across our website are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) with 256-bit cryptographic keys. Our internal databases are protected behind multi-tier firewalls with strict role-based access controls.

Our server architecture undergoes regular vulnerability assessments, automated intrusion detection monitoring, and strict internal access auditing to ensure your information remains shielded at all times.

6. Individual Privacy Rights and Global Protections

Regardless of your geographic location, CottonKnitPod extends comprehensive individual privacy rights to all users:

  • Right to Access: You may request a complete portable copy of all personal records we maintain regarding your identity and order history.
  • Right to Rectification: You may correct inaccurate or incomplete biographical or shipping details.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You may request the permanent deletion of your customer profile, subject to statutory accounting retention constraints.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You may limit how we process your information in certain contested circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format for transfer to another provider.
  • Right to Object: Object at any time to the processing of your data for direct communication or analytical telemetry.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Exercising your privacy rights will never result in altered pricing, reduced service quality, or denied access to our artisanal products.

7. Third-Party Service Providers & Data Transfers

We collaborate exclusively with trusted third-party service providers who demonstrate verifiable compliance with rigorous data protection standards. These include international logistics couriers (FedEx, UPS, DHL), cloud infrastructure hosts, and payment processors. When data is transferred internationally, we utilize standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

8. Children's Online Privacy Protection

Our website and services are not directed to individuals under sixteen years of age. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children. If we discover that personal data from a minor has been collected without verified parental consent, we take immediate steps to delete the information from our servers.

10. Global Cross-Border Data Flows and Adequacy Frameworks

Because CottonKnitPod serves an international community of textile connoisseurs across North America, the United Kingdom, the European Union, Australia, and East Asia, personal data may be transferred across international borders. When data originates from the European Economic Area (EEA) or Switzerland and is processed within United States cloud data centers, we implement European Commission-approved Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) alongside rigorous Technical and Organizational Measures (TOMs) to guarantee an equivalent level of data protection.

Our transatlantic data pipelines utilize end-to-end cryptographic hashing, TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, and AES-256 encryption at rest. We periodically review third-party hosting partners, fulfillment dispatch nodes, and payment gateways to ensure strict adherence to ISO/IEC 27001 information security management benchmarks.

11. Data Breach Incident Notification & Disaster Recovery

CottonKnitPod maintains an active Cybersecurity Incident Response Team (CSIRT) and comprehensive business continuity protocols. In the unlikely event of a verified data security breach impacting customer personal records, our data protection team initiates automated containment workflows, conducts forensic impact assessments, and notifies affected individuals and relevant supervisory data protection authorities within seventy-two (72) hours of confirmed discovery, in full accordance with GDPR Article 33 guidelines.

Notification dispatches will outline the nature of the breach, the specific data categories involved, the mitigation actions taken by our security engineers, and practical steps patrons can take to protect their credentials.
